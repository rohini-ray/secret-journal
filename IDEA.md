## The idea

People still need a quiet place that is not a public feed. Noctuary is a
digital secret journal that feels like a physical book: a cover you dress
yourself, ten inner pages, and a lock that stays on your own device.

The cover is the ritual. Before writing, the keeper of the journal
arranges stars in different geometries, coloured flowers, and small
emoji charms. That cover is unique to them — a visual password of mood,
not of login.

Inside, there are at most ten pages. The limit is deliberate. A secret
is meant to be chosen, not dumped. Entries never leave the browser
unless the writer decides to share.

Privacy is the default. Nothing is uploaded to a server. The journal
lives in local storage on the writer’s machine. If they want someone
trusted to read it, Noctuary builds a share link that carries an
encrypted snapshot in the URL itself. Anyone without that link cannot
see the pages. The writer can also revoke the habit of sharing simply
by not sending the link, and by keeping the original only on their
device.

## What it is for

- Late-night thoughts that should not sit in a chat app
- Letters to a future self
- A private garden of words with a cover that feels handmade

## What it is not

- A social network
- A cloud diary with accounts and ads
- An unlimited notebook
