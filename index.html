Noctuary — a secret journal. Ten pages. Yours alone unless you share.

The idea

Noctuary is a quiet book for thoughts that do not belong on a feed. You dress the cover with stars of different geometries, coloured flowers, and small charms. Inside there are ten pages — enough for a secret, not enough to become a dump.

The journal never leaves your device unless you create a share link. That link holds an encrypted snapshot in the address itself, so there is no public archive of other people’s writing.
